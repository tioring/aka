This year, we...

1997.09.01
Started senior year. Mr. Park gave a speech that lasted 47 minutes. We timed it.
1997.10.18
Field trip to Gyeongju. Three students fell asleep at the Bulguksa pagoda. (No names.)
1997.12.24
School concert — orchestra played Pachelbel's Canon for the 11th year in a row. We're sorry. Pachelbel is sorry.
1998.03.14
Sport's day. Hyewon ran the 200m faster than the math teacher. Photo evidence on page 38.
1998.04.22
Talent show — Jin played Eric Clapton's Tears in Heaven on three drums. It worked, somehow.
1998.06.18
Prom. Three couples danced the cha-cha incorrectly but with conviction. Mr. Park danced better than expected.
1998.07.12
Last day. Graduation. We threw our hats. Two were never recovered.
