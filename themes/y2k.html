★ ABOUT ME ★ updated 2026·04·14

about this corner

hi i'm kim, i live somewhere with too much rain and i make small things on the internet. this site is hand-coded in plain html bc i think CMSes are for cowards and also bc i can't afford a CMS.

i like typography, dip pens, late 90s rpg soundtracks, and overengineered grocery lists. i don't like uppercase letters or talking on the phone or websites that make you scroll for ten minutes to find the price.

this page has been online since may 2003. it had three different themes before this one. each was uglier than the last and i stand by that.
