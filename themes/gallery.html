— Curatorial note

Choi has been working with the same set of three colors for thirty-one years. He calls them, with the matter-of-factness that has always characterized his speech, the morning, the wall, and the shadow. The colors do not change. The light does. Threshold is, in part, a study of the latter.

The twelve works gathered here were made between 2018 and 2025. They are arranged not chronologically — Choi is uninterested in chronology — but according to a sequence the artist suggests is "the order in which a person walks into a room and notices, at different speeds, that something has been moved."

This is not a quiet exhibition. It is, however, an exhibition that asks to be approached at the rate of walking.

— Soomin Yang, curator. April 2026
